Subject: Econometrics 

1) "An Econometric Analysis of Rajasthan’s State Domestic Product: The Role of Agriculture and Manufacturing"
   
   Analyzed Rajasthan’s State Domestic Product (1980–2023) using descriptive statistics and log-linear regression in R, examining the contributions of agriculture and manufacturing across three economic periods. Found significant positive associations for both sectors, with manufacturing exhibiting the larger estimated coefficient.
   
   https://docs.google.com/document/d/1rojG69C1H1dGIZwTgqkMaLQtFGJrVHoU9ODXJakJtCM/edit?usp=sharing


2) "Regression Analysis of Direct and Indirect Tax Contribution to India's Revenue Receipts"
   
   Studied the composition and trends of India's tax revenue receipts, examining why indirect taxes outweigh direct taxes despite the Ramsey Rule's recommendation, using descriptive statistics and correlation analysis. Built a log-linear regression model in R showing indirect tax has a stronger effect on total revenue (β=0.71) than direct tax (β=0.28), with the model explaining 99.99% of the variance.
   
   https://docs.google.com/document/d/11B3SVpb5KTSNNQFMvrucSR5_zdz2JWt1/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=113897165556347456641&rtpof=true&sd=true


3) "Time Series Analysis of Sweden's Trade Balance: Autocorrelation, Heteroscedasticity, and Multicollinearity Diagnostics (1960–2023)"

   Analyzed Sweden's export-import trade balance from 1960–2023 using World Bank data, running diagnostic tests for autocorrelation (Durbin-Watson), heteroscedasticity (Breusch-Pagan), and multicollinearity (VIF) on regression models. Found severe multicollinearity between exports and imports (VIF ≈ 378) alongside significant positive autocorrelation, highlighting key OLS assumption violations despite a near-perfect model fit (adj. R² = 0.997).

   https://docs.google.com/document/d/1_2FO82U_OIoGULZd89cWIlV0Kzv8uKm_wP5w3OAXmuA/edit?usp=sharing



Subject: Advanced Macroeconomics 

1) "Masterstroke Gone Awry: An Evaluation of India's 2016 Demonetization Policy"\
   
   Evaluated the short, medium, and long-term economic impact of India's 2016 demonetization policy against its 7 stated objectives (curbing black money, fake currency, terror financing, digital adoption, tax compliance, financial inclusion). Concluded the policy largely failed on its core goals — 99.3% of demonetized currency returned to banks and cash usage rebounded by 2019 — while disproportionately harming the informal sector and rural economy.

1) "Meghalaya’s Revenue Receipts and Revenue Expenditure over the years: Analysis"
   
   Analyzed Meghalaya’s revenue receipts and expenditure over 51 years using trend analysis, fiscal ratios, GSVA trends and OLS regression. Examined the determinants of revenue balance and found significant relationships between revenue receipts, expenditure and the state’s fiscal position.

2) "Healthcare Expenditure Analysis of Assam, Odisha & Chhattisgarh — A Regression-Based Study"

   Analyzed the relationship between revenue expenditure, capital expenditure, and total health expenditure across Assam, Odisha, and Chhattisgarh using log-linear regression models in R. Found that revenue expenditure is the dominant driver of total health spending in all three states (R² > 0.999), with capital expenditure playing a smaller but statistically significant role.

3) "Comparative Analysis of Mission Antyodaya's Rural Development Impact in Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh"

   Compared rural development outcomes across health, education, agriculture, and public distribution systems in Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh using Mission Antyodaya data (2019–2023) alongside state budget and SDG performance metrics. Found Maharashtra leads in health infrastructure and agricultural diversification while UP outperforms in food security outreach, concluding that state-specific policy customization — rather than uniform national schemes — is key to closing rural development gaps.

Subject: Behavioral Economics 

1) Book Review — "Predictably Irrational" by Dan Ariely

   Reviewed Dan Ariely's "Predictably Irrational," summarizing key behavioral economics concepts including the decoy effect, the zero-price effect, the endowment effect, loss aversion, and the placebo effect of pricing, with real-world examples across marketing, finance, and consumer behavior. Subject: Environmental Economics 
1) "Quantifying the Economic Impact of Air Pollution on Public Health in the Philippines (2006–2016)"

   Analyzed 2006-2016 mortality, CO2, and greenhouse gas data for the Philippines using log-linear regression, finding a 1% rise in CO2 emissions associated with a 0.98% increase in deaths, versus a much weaker 0.06% effect from greenhouse gases. Estimated the associated economic cost of pollution-linked deaths at ~$1.84 billion in lost lifetime wages, concluding that stricter environmental regulation and cleaner technology investment are needed.

   https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Mo5HrHMhmom3BFOiBYLCVKrTj9NIpIYmFImvOAnWlwA/edit?usp=sharing

2) "The Efficacy of Renewable Energy Policy on Reducing CO2 Emissions and Economic Development — India & Norway"

   Comparatively analyzed renewable energy policy, carbon pricing, and Environmental Kuznets Curve dynamics in India and Norway using regression models on 25+ years of CO2, GDP, tax, and subsidy data. Found Norway's carbon taxes and renewable growth significantly reduce emissions while already past its Kuznets turning point, whereas India's subsidies were statistically linked to increased emissions, reflecting its earlier, fossil-fuel-dependent stage of development.

Subject: Money, Banking and Financial Institutions
1) Impact of the 2008 Global Financial Crisis on Canada — A Macro-Financial Indicator Analysis

   Analyzed the impact of the 2008 Global Financial Crisis on Canada through key macro-financial indicators — 1-year T-bill yields, the Bank of Canada's overnight rate, inflation, and credit default spreads. Found the crisis caused a 3.3% GDP contraction, 8.7% unemployment, and a ~15-20% housing price decline, with government stimulus ($63B) and rate cuts cushioning but not preventing a slow, uneven recovery lasting until 2013.
